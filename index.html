PATIENCE & CRAFT

The 48-Hour Journey

Great bread cannot be rushed. From the first mix to the final bake, every loaf is given the time it needs to develop its character.

01 — MIX & REST
0 Hours

Flour & Water

Flour, water, starter, and salt come together before the dough begins its slow transformation.

02 — SLOW FERMENT
24 Hours

Flavor Development

Time develops the flavor, aroma, and open texture that define naturally fermented sourdough.

03 — BAKE & BLOOM
48 Hours

Stone Oven Bake

Each loaf is hand-shaped, scored, and baked until deeply golden with a crisp crust and tender crumb.
